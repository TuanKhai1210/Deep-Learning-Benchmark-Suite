  <section id="problem" class="study-section"><span class="eyebrow">ASSIGNMENT 3 / PROBLEM STATEMENT</span><h2>When does the image improve product classification?</h2><p>The team compares image-only and title-only classifiers with multimodal models under a shared split and evaluation protocol.</p></section>

  <section id="data" class="study-section"><span class="eyebrow">02 / DATASET DESCRIPTION &amp; EDA</span><h2>Provenance before model choice.</h2><p>The source metadata yielded 7,221 candidate image–title pairs across ten product types. The team is reviewing pair quality and product groups for the final split.</p></section>

  <section id="method" class="study-section"><span class="eyebrow">03 / METHODOLOGY</span><h2>Single-modality baselines and fusion.</h2><p>Frozen ResNet-18 and DistilBERT produce image and title features. We compare concatenation, concatenation with an elementwise product, and a matched ablation that uses the elementwise mean.</p></section>

  <section id="setup" class="study-section"><span class="eyebrow">04 / EXPERIMENTAL SETUP</span><h2>One protocol for every condition.</h2><p>All models use the same reviewed split and select their checkpoints by validation macro-F1. We record the seed, encoder versions, cache identity and hardware for each run.</p></section>

  <section id="results" class="study-section"><span class="eyebrow">05 / RESULTS</span><h2>Results will follow verified artifacts.</h2><div class="result-placeholder"><span class="status-tag">PILOT COMPLETE</span><h3>Final benchmark pending.</h3><p>A 300-pair CPU pilot exercised all three fusion variants across three seeds. The final comparison will use the reviewed product-group split and both unimodal baselines.</p></div></section>

  <section id="discussion" class="study-section"><span class="eyebrow">06 / COMPARISON, DISCUSSION &amp; ERROR ANALYSIS</span><h2>Test the value of each modality.</h2><p>The future analysis will compare unimodal and multimodal systems under matched splits and seeds, examine modality agreement and disagreement, and inspect failures where one or both modalities are weak, missing, noisy, or misleading.</p></section>

  <section id="limitations" class="study-section"><span class="eyebrow">07 / LIMITATIONS &amp; CONCLUSION</span><h2>Publish uncertainty explicitly.</h2><p>Dataset and modality limitations, alignment assumptions, access restrictions, missing-data effects, and compute scope will be stated in the final conclusion. No expected model quality or fusion benefit is represented as an observed result.</p></section>
