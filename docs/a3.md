  <section id="problem" class="study-section"><span class="eyebrow">ASSIGNMENT 3 / PROBLEM STATEMENT</span><h2>When do images and titles improve product classification?</h2><p>The team compares image-only and title-only classifiers with multimodal models under a shared split and evaluation protocol.</p></section>

  <section id="data" class="study-section"><h2>Dataset and split</h2><p>The reviewed Amazon Berkeley Objects cohort has 7,216 aligned catalog pairs across ten product types. Train, validation and test have 5,052, 1,083 and 1,081 listings respectively. Each reviewed product group stays in one split.</p></section>

  <section id="method" class="study-section"><span class="eyebrow">03 / METHODOLOGY</span><h2>Single-modality baselines and fusion.</h2><p>Frozen ResNet-18 and DistilBERT produce image and title features. We compare concatenation, concatenation with an elementwise product, and a matched ablation that uses the elementwise mean.</p></section>

  <section id="setup" class="study-section"><span class="eyebrow">04 / EXPERIMENTAL SETUP</span><h2>One protocol for every condition.</h2><p>All models use the same reviewed split and select their checkpoints by validation macro-F1. We record the seed, encoder versions, cache identity and hardware for each run.</p></section>

  <section id="results" class="study-section">

  <section id="discussion" class="study-section">
    <h2>What the two inputs contribute</h2>
    <p>Concat improves macro-F1 by 3.69 points over image-only, the validation-selected unimodal reference. Product gains 0.83 points over concat and 1.01 points over an equal-size mean fusion head. In seed 67, product raises macro-F1 by 0.08 points while getting three fewer listings correct than concat.</p>
  </section>
  <section id="limitations" class="study-section">
    <h2>Limits of the comparison</h2>
    <p>The cohort contains ten catalog categories from one marketplace and uses one reviewed product-group split. The benchmarks require both modalities for fusion. The reported CPU test timing starts from cached features.</p>
  </section>
